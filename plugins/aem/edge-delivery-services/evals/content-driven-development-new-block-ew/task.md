# Add a plans block

We need a `plans` block for our pricing page. It shows one card per plan: the plan name, the monthly price, the price per month when billed annually, a short feature list and a call-to-action link. Visitors flip a Monthly / Annual toggle. The cards show the price for the chosen period and, in annual mode, how much cheaper annual is than monthly, as a whole percentage.

This project is authored in Experience Workspace. The site's content is cloned into `./content/`, and `aem up` serves it locally.
