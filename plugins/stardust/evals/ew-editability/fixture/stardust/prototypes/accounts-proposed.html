Rates and fees

The questions behind the numbers

How is APY different from the dividend rate?

APY counts monthly compounding, so it is the number you actually earn over a year. We quote APY everywhere so you never have to convert.

What happens to my savings rate if the market rate drops?

Tide Savings is variable and can move with the market. We announce any change by e-mail and on this page at least ten days before it takes effect.

Is there really no fee on Free Checking?

No monthly fee, no minimum, no overdraft fee. The only charges are for things you ask for, such as a stop payment or a cashier's cheque, and those are listed on the fee schedule.

Can I hold a certificate jointly or in a trust?

Yes. Joint, custodial and revocable-trust ownership are all available at any branch, and each ownership category carries its own federal insurance limit.
