Sample Lane, by the old mill

Visit the store

The door is green and the bell is loud. Come in, put your bag down and ask what arrived this week.

Getting here

Ten minutes on foot from the station: follow the river, cross at the footbridge, second door after the bakery. There are bicycle stands in the yard.
