Good to know

Questions members ask

Who can join Meridian Coast Credit Union?

Anyone who lives, works, worships or studies in one of the twelve coastal towns we serve, plus their family members. A $5 share deposit makes you a member-owner.

Are my deposits insured?

Yes. Deposits are federally insured up to $250,000 per member, per ownership category, the same protection a bank account carries.

Do you charge ATM fees?

Not at our machines or the shared co-operative network of more than 30,000 ATMs nationwide. Other operators may add their own surcharge, and we refund up to $10 of those each month on Free Checking.

How fast is a loan decision?

Auto and personal loans are usually decided the same business day. Mortgages take longer because a local underwriter reviews every file, typically two to three weeks to close.

Can I bank entirely from my phone?

You can open accounts, deposit cheques, move money, freeze a card and message an advisor in the app. The only things that still need a branch visit are notarised documents and safe deposit boxes.

What happens to the profit?

There are no shareholders. After reserves are set aside, earnings return to members as higher savings rates, lower loan rates and the fees we do not charge.
