# Direction — Meridian Coast Credit Union

**Phrase (verbatim):** "keep our colours and voice, but make the site feel like a modern coastal credit union instead of a bank form"

**Mode:** A — brand-faithful. The captured surface is signal-strong (deep teal / sand / coral palette, serif display type, plain-spoken member voice); the redesign moves composition and rhythm, not identity.

**IA fidelity:** verbatim — page set and section order stay; presentation changes.

**Resolved tokens (exposed on `:root` in every prototype):** heading `"Iowan Old Style", Georgia, serif`; body system sans; ground sand `#f4efe6`; ink `#14212b`; brand teal `#0b4f6c`; accent coral `#e4572e`; 8px spacing scale; 1200px container; 12px radius.

**Voice rules kept:** second person, no jargon, rates and fees stated plainly next to the product they belong to.

**Anti-toolbox audit:** no gradient text, no glass panels, no side stripes; one accent per band.
