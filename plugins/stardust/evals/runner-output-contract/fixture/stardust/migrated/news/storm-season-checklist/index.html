Storm season checklist: five things to do before the first warning

4 May 2026 · Claims team

Every spring our adjusters see the same five losses that a weekend of preparation would have prevented. Here is the list they asked us to publish.

1. Photograph every room

A contents claim settles in days when there is a dated photo record and in weeks when there is not.

2. Clear the gutters and check the sump pump

Water that cannot leave the roof finds its way into the house. Test the pump by pouring a bucket into the pit.

3. Know your deductible

Wind and hail deductibles are often a percentage of the dwelling limit, not a flat amount. Your declarations page shows which applies.

4. Trim what overhangs the roof

Limbs within ten feet of the roofline are the most common cause of storm damage we pay.

5. Save the claims number now

1-800-555-0142 answers around the clock during a declared weather event.
