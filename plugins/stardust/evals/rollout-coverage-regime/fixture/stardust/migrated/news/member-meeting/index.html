2025 annual report: surplus, claims paid, and what comes next

18 March 2026 · Office of the president

Policyholder surplus ended the year at $212 million, up 4.1 percent. We paid $61.4 million in claims, 38 percent of it within a week of first notice.

Where the premium went

Sixty-eight cents of every premium dollar returned to members as claims. Operating expense held at 27 cents; the remainder went to surplus.

What comes next

The 2026 plan funds a second claims office in the north county and keeps rates flat on home policies for the third consecutive year.
