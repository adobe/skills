How it actually works

It learns your taste. Here's how.

Not a preset menu, and not a guess. The Atelier works the way a good barista does — it pays attention, remembers, and adjusts. Four quiet steps, running every morning.

1

It tastes every cup

Sensors read the grind, the flow, the strength and the temperature of every pour — the same things a barista would notice.

2

It builds your flavour DNA

Those readings become a profile for each person in the house, refined a little more with every cup you pour.

3

It reads the morning

The time, your calendar, the weather, and who's awake all shape what it suggests — before you've asked for anything.

4

It acts on its own

It warms up before your alarm, adjusts the pour to match, and reorders beans before you run low. You just lift the cup.

Your flavour DNA and morning routines stay on your machine and your account. You can review what it has learned, correct it, or wipe it clean, any time.
